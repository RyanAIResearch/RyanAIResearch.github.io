OpenFold3 · NVIDIA H100 80GB · single GPU

Custom triangle kernels that train OpenFold3 faster, and learn the same thing.

StableFold replaces the triangle-attention and triangle-multiplicative path in OpenFold3. Measured against three production backends across the three crop sizes of the official training recipe, 400 steps each, every arm fine-tuning from the released of3-p2-155k weights — twelve runs on one H100.

Power

Board power draw

The harness samples the GPU with nvidia-smi every 10 seconds for the whole of each run. Below is that log plotted as recorded — every sample, no smoothing, no fitting. Hover a curve for the reading at that moment.

Each curve starts when its own process starts, so the horizontal extent is the run length — StableFold's line stops first. Draw swings by hundreds of watts inside a single training step, which is why the traces read as bands rather than lines; that texture is the measurement, not noise added in plotting.

Power is a wash against cuEquivariance. Mean draw is 221 vs 207 W at crop 384, 280 vs 279 W at 640, and 311 vs 314 W at 768 — no consistent winner. StableFold finishes sooner at every crop, but it does not get there by drawing less. Against DeepSpeed the gap is real (320 vs 280 W at 640, 361 vs 311 W at 768).

Numerical parity

The loss curves land on top of each other

A faster kernel is only useful if the model still trains the same way. All four backends ran from an identical seed and dataset order, so their loss trajectories should agree to within run-to-run noise. Hover a chart to read every backend at that step.

The gaps above are all between 0.008 and 0.011, roughly 0.6–0.8% of the loss itself, and final validation loss lands between 0.0685 and 0.0693 across every arm that completed. Three independent implementations trace one trajectory.

AQLab Triton diverges on the larger crops. Under exactly this protocol it went to NaN at step 29 on crop 640 and step 10 on crop 768, and never recovered; those two cells have no training time and no loss curve to report. It completed crop 384, but even there its agreement with StableFold (0.031) is three times looser than cuEquivariance or DeepSpeed. Two independent divergences, earlier as the crop grows — a reproducible failure mode, not a one-off.

How far this evidence reaches. Every cell is a single run, so there are no error bars; the 1.4–4.5% margins against cuEquivariance are within the range a repeat could move, and only the direction — StableFold ahead at all three crops — is firm. The 400-step window also sits inside the 1000-step learning-rate warmup, so peak learning rate is never reached. Repeats and a run past warmup are the next measurements.
